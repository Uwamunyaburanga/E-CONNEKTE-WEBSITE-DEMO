ABOUT E-CONNEKTE

Student needs.
Shared solutions.

You need, I need — Let's Connect!

E-CONNEKTE is a student-centred platform designed to make everyday student life easier through connection and sharing.

From travelling between cities and finding affordable essentials to settling into a new place and studying with others, our goal is to bring useful student services together in one welcoming community.

We are starting with a pilot community in Cesena and building the platform step by step with students.
