One platform for three everyday student needs.

Students often need affordable ways to travel, furnish a room and settle into an unfamiliar city. E-CONNEKTE is designed to connect students with one another and make those everyday tasks easier. We are developing the idea with students and local partners, and want their feedback to shape what comes next.
